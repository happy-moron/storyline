Boring Thomas lived in a small house.
He lived with his cat, Frank.  
Thomas was not a brave man.
Even small things made him upset.
One day, Thomas ran out of milk.
He opened his fridge.
He looked at the bottom shelf.
The milk bottle was empty.
"Oh bother," said Thomas. "Such a calamity!"
"When will I have time to buy more milk?"
"I have to arrive at work at eight in the morning."
"I won't get home until 6PM."
"If I go to the store after work, I will get home at 630PM."
"I won't want to go."
"I will be tired."
"But I should go."
"I need milk."
"Maybe I can drink my coffee black," thought Thomas.
Just then, Frank came up to Thomas.
He touched Thomas's leg with his paw.
He was telling Thomas that they needed milk.
Thomas looked at Frank.
He hoped that Frank was telling him something.
He hoped that someone was at the door.
He hoped they had milk.
Maybe he had another bottle of milk.
Maybe it was at the back of the fridge.
He could check behind the lettuce.
Behind the lettuce was an old carrot.
There was no milk.
Thomas looked at his cupboard.
He couldn't remember if he had any powdered milk.
There was some pasta in his cupboard.
There was some tomato paste. 
There was some salt.
There wasn't any powdered milk.
There was some corn starch.
Thomas had an idea.
He took a spoon from a drawer.
He took the corn starch out of the cupboard.
He put the corn starch down on the kitchen counter
Thomas went to the cupboard.
He got out a glass.
He filled the glass with water.
Then, he stirred a big spoon of corn-starch into the water.
The water turned white. 
It looked just like milk.
Frank meowed.
"It's okay," said Thomas. "You can't tell the difference."
Frank jumped up onto the counter.
He knocked over the glass.
The 'milk' spilled onto the floor.
Thomas got a rag.
Thomas wiped up the spilled milk.
Thomas looked at Frank.
"Meow," said Frank.
Thomas looked at his watch.
He could go to the supermarket.

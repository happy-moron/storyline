Thomas woke up at 7:30AM.
It was a sunny saturday morning.
His cat Frank was sitting on the bed.
"Feed me," Frank said.
To Thomas, it sounded like "Meow."
But Thomas understood what Frank was saying.
Frank said the same thing every morning.
He said it at the same time every morning.
Frank didn't want to eat only on some mornings.
Frank wanted to eat every morning.

"All right, all right, I'll feed you," said Thomas.
"But first things first."
Thomas wanted to go get his mail.
He was expecting an exciting letter.
It was almost time to renew his cell phone plan.
He didn't want to miss the renewal letter.
Maybe he could get the same plan again at a slightly lower cost.
So exciting!

Thomas opened his door.
He stepped onto his front steps.
Thomas stopped.
He saw something in the flower bed next to the front steps.
It was garbage!
It looked like an old candy wrapper.
Thomas was angry.
"The flower bed belongs to the flowers," he thought.
Thomas didn't want to pick up the garbage with his bare hands.
Thomas had an idea.
He went back inside to get some gloves.
He was so upset, he forgot to close the door.

It took Thomas a little while to find the gloves.
Finally, he found them. 
He put on the gloves.
He went outside and picked up the garbage.
He went back inside.
He put the garbage into the garbage can.
"That's one thing done," he thought.

Thomas had forgotten about the letter.
He was in the kitchen.
"It's time to feed Frank," he thought.
"Where is Frank?"
Usually, Frank would come and rub against his legs.
Usually, Frank would ask loudly to be fed.
Usually, Thomas would have no peace until Frank was fed.
But the kitchen was quiet.

Thomas got some cat food from the cupboard.
He put it into a bowl.
He put the bowl on the floor.
He poured some fresh water into another bowl.
He put the water bowl beside the food bowl.
"Frank," called Thomas, "It's time for breakfast!"

Thomas expected to hear Frank running into the kitchen.
But Thomas didn't hear anything.
It was quiet.

Now Thomas was worried.
He called again, but there was no answer.
Something wasn't right.
Frank never missed breakfast.

Thomas searched the whole house.
He searched the bedroom.
He searched the living room.
He searched the bathroom.
He searched the kitchen.
He searched the hallway.
He looked under the table and under the chairs.
He looked under the bed.
He looked behind the couch.
He looked in the washing machine.
He looked in the closet.
He stood and listened very carefully.
He did this in every room.
Everything was quiet.
Frank was gone!

Thomas was suddenly very, very scared.
What had happened?

That is right. Today we are learning how to say "before" and "after" in Chinese. In HSK grammar, this means using the words 以前 and 以后.

en=student
So, if I want to say "after three o'clock" or "before eating," I use these two?

en=teacher
Exactly. But pay attention to the word order! In English, we say "before" or "after" first, like "before lunch." In Chinese, the time or the action always comes first. You say "lunch before" or "three o'clock after."

en=student
Wait, so it is [Time/Action] plus 以前 or 以后?

en=teacher
Spot on! For example, if you want to say "after eating," you say 吃 以后. Or if you want to say "before the beach," you say 沙滩 以前.

en=student
Got it. So, if I say 游泳 以前, I am saying "before swimming."

en=teacher
Perfect. Today's vocabulary includes things you would definitely find at the beach: 太阳 meaning sun, 防晒霜 meaning sunscreen, 游泳 meaning to swim, 冲浪 meaning to surf, and 潜水 meaning to dive.

en=student

en=teacher

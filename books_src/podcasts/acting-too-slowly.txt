en=teacher
Exactly. First, let's look at our grammar. When you want to emphasize that a time has already arrived or a limit has been reached, you use the structure 都...了. For example, 都十二点了 (It's already twelve o'clock). The 都 adds a sense of "it's already at this point," which often carries a tone of complaint or urgency.

en=student
Okay, so it's like emphasizing the extremity of the situation. And what about 再?

en=teacher
再 is used for actions that will happen again in the future. A common way to use it is in the phrase 以后再... (yǐ hòu zài...), meaning "do [something] later" or "do [something] again later." It's for future intentions. It is very different from 又, which we use for things that have already happened again.

en=student
So if I missed the bus and I'm saying "I'll take the next one," I'd use 再?

en=teacher
Precisely. Now, before we hear the dialogue, let's go over our key vocabulary.

en=teacher

en=teacher
We have 迟到, (second tone and fourth tone), meaning to be late... 迟到.

en=teacher
We have 以后, (third tone and fourth tone), meaning later or afterwards... 以后.

en=teacher
We have 磨蹭, (second tone and light tone), meaning to dawdle or loiter... 磨蹭.

en=teacher
We have 动作, (fourth tone and fourth tone), meaning movement or action... 动作.

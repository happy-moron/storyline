en=teacher
Exactly. In a high-pressure environment, like a busy restaurant kitchen, being slow can cause real drama. Today, we are learning two essential grammar points to express this. 

en=teacher
First, the structure A is B. You use this to define something or identify what a problem is. For example, 我是老师, meaning I am a teacher, or 问题是这个, meaning the problem is this.

en=student
So, it is the standard way to say what something is. What about the second point?

en=teacher
The second point is expressing excessiveness using the pattern 太 plus an adjective plus 了. This is how you say something is "too" much of a certain quality. For example, 太大了, meaning too big, or 太贵了, meaning too expensive. It's a very common way to complain!

en=student
Ah, so the 了 at the end is crucial for that "too" feeling. Without it, it's just a description.

en=teacher
Precisely. Now, let's look at our six key vocabulary words for today. 

en=teacher
First, we have 动作, which is fourth tone, fourth tone, meaning movement or action. 动作.

en=teacher
Next, 慢, which is fourth tone, meaning slow. 慢.

en=teacher
Then, 准备, which is third tone, fourth tone, meaning to prepare. 准备.

en=teacher
We also have 问题, which is fourth tone, second tone, meaning problem. 问题.

en=teacher
Next is 厨房, which is second tone, second tone, meaning kitchen. 厨房.

en=teacher
And finally, 速度, which is fourth tone, fourth tone, meaning speed. 速度.

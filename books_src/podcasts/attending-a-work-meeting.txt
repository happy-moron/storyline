BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's look at that intense dialogue and break it down line by line. We start with the manager, Speaker One.

zh=1
这是我们今年最重要的会议，大家必须认真对待。
This is our most important meeting this year; everyone must take it seriously.

en=student
"最重要的" (zuì zhòng yào de). "重要" means important. So "最" makes it "the most important." I noticed there is a "的" at the end there. Is that always necessary?

en=teacher
When "最" plus an adjective is describing a noun, like "会议" (meeting), we almost always use "的" to connect them. It functions like "the most important meeting."

en=student
Got it. And "认真对待" means "to take seriously."

en=teacher
Correct. Now, let's look at Speaker Two, who sounds a little nervous.

zh=2
那个...那个广告的预算...好像不太够。
That... that advertisement budget... seems not quite enough.

en=student
There it is! "那个...那个..." (nèige... nèige...). He is clearly hesitating. He sounds like he is afraid to deliver bad news.

en=teacher
Exactly. He is using "那个" as a filler word while he searches for the right way to say "the budget is not enough." Also, note "预算" (yù suàn), which means budget. And "不太够" means "not quite enough."

en=student
"不太" is a great way to soften a sentence, right? Instead of saying "not enough," saying "not quite enough" sounds a bit more polite.

en=teacher
Very observant. Now, Speaker Three enters, and she is definitely not a fan of the current situation.

zh=3
不够？这简直是我们做的最失败的一次推广！
Insufficient? This is simply our most failed promotion!

en=student
"最失败" (zuì shī bài). "失败" is to fail or failure, so "most failed" or "the biggest failure." And she used "简直" (jiǎn zhí), which means "simply" or "at all." She is very passionate!

en=teacher
She is definitely the most direct person in the room. Let's look at the next line from Speaker Two.

zh=2
但是...那个客户的要求...太高了。
But... that client's requirements... are too high.

en=student
"要求" (yāo qiú) means requirements or demands. And "太高了" (tài gāo le) means too high. It seems this client is very difficult.

en=teacher
Indeed. Let's go back to the manager, who is trying to act as the peacemaker.

zh=1
大家冷静点。现在的困难确实是最大的。
Everyone, calm down. The current difficulties are indeed the greatest.

en=student
"冷静点" (lěng jìng diǎn) means "calm down a bit." And she says "最大的困难" (zuì dà de kùn nán) — the greatest difficulty. 

en=teacher
Yes. "困难" (kùn nán) is difficulty. She also uses "确实" (què shí), which means "indeed" or "really." It adds emphasis to her acknowledgement of the problem.

en=student
Then Speaker Three comes back with a very specific concern.

zh=3
我最担心的还是执行力的问题。
What I am most worried about is the problem of execution.

en=teacher
"最担心的" (zuì dān xīn de). "担心" is to worry. So "the thing I worry about most."

en=student
"执行力" (zhí xíng lì) is an interesting word. It looks like "execution" and "power/ability."

en=teacher
Precisely. In a business context, "执行力" is a very common term referring to how well a team can actually carry out a plan.

en=student
Then Speaker Two asks about a deadline.

zh=2
那个...那个方案...我们明天能定下来吗？
That... that plan... can we finalize it tomorrow?

en=teacher
"方案" (fāng àn) is a plan or a proposal. And "定下来" (dìng xià lái) is a very useful phrasal verb meaning to finalize, to settle, or to decide something once and for all.

en=student
Speaker Three doesn't want to wait!

zh=3
明天太晚了，我们要立刻行动！
Tomorrow is too late, we need to act immediately!

en=teacher
"立刻" (lì kè) means immediately. No hesitation there!

en=student
And finally, the manager tries to end on a positive note.

zh=1
没关系，我们今天一定要找到最好的解决办法。
It is okay, we must find the best solution today.

en=student
"解决办法" (jiě jué bàn fǎ). "解决" is to solve, and "办法" is a way or method. So "solution." And "最好的" is our superlative again — the best.

en=teacher
You've got it. This conversation shows how "最" can express the extremes of a situation, and how "那个" can help you navigate those awkward silences when the pressure is on.

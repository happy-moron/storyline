en=student
I felt that frustration! Before we go, can I ask about the difference between 有点儿 and 一点儿? I always get them mixed up.

en=teacher
That is a classic learner's struggle! Think of it this way: 有点儿 is usually placed *before* the adjective to express a negative or unpleasant feeling. "有点儿贵" (a little too expensive). But if you want to say "a little bit of something" or compare something, you often use 一点儿 *after* the adjective. For example, "便宜一点儿" means "a little bit cheaper."

en=student

en=teacher
Exactly! You've got it. And remember the distance rule: A 离 B 远 or 近. Don't forget the 离!

Great question. In Chinese, we usually say "be interested in [something]." So we use the structure 对 [something] 感兴趣. Here, the "something" is what, which is 什么.

zh=1
我对历史很感兴趣。你能帮我看看吗？
I am very interested in history. Can you help me look?

en=student
She uses 历史 (lì shǐ) for history. And then she asks "Can you help me look?". 看看 (kàn kan) is the verb 见 (kàn) doubled.

en=teacher
Spot on. Because Li Hua wants the book to come toward her, she uses 来. The 吧 at the end makes the request sound like a suggestion or a gentle command rather than an order.

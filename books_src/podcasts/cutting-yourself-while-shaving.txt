BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's look at that dialogue, and break it down line by line. It starts with Xiao Ming in pain.

zh=1
哎哟！太疼了！
Ouch! It hurts so much!

en=student
"哎哟" is that classic sound for "ouch" or "oh no." And "疼" is the second tone we mentioned earlier. 

en=teacher
Correct. Then Xiao Hua asks what is happening.

zh=2
你怎么了？你在做什么？
What's wrong? What are you doing?

en=student
"你怎么了" is a very common way to ask "What's wrong with you?" or "What happened to you?"

en=teacher
Right. And then we get to the core of the problem.

zh=1
我在刮胡子，但是我不小心切到脸了。
I am shaving, but I accidentally cut my face.

en=student
Here is our first big grammar point! "我不小心." "不" is the negation, and "小心" means careful. So "I am not careful" or "I accidentally..." did that mean it's a single word?

en=teacher
It can be used as a set phrase meaning "accidentally" or "carelessly." It's "不" plus "小心." And "刮胡子" is our vocab word for shaving.

en=student
Then Xiao Hua reacts. She seems pretty stressed.

zh=2
天哪！你看，一直在出血！你都受伤了，怎么还不小心？
Oh my god! Look, it's bleeding! You are already injured, how are you still so careless?

en=teacher
"出血" is our verb for bleeding. But look at the sentence "你都受伤了". Here, "都" is being used to emphasize that the situation is already at a certain point—"You've already injured yourself."

en=student
Wait, I thought "都" was just for "all" or "both"?

en=teacher
In this specific context, it adds emphasis to the state of being injured, similar to "already." But let's look at how we use "不" again in the next line.

zh=1
我觉得没事，我不觉得这很严重。
I think it is fine, I don't think this is serious.

en=student
"我不觉得" is "I don't think." So it's "不" plus "觉得," which means to feel or think.

en=teacher
Exactly. Xiao Hua is definitely not agreeing with him.

zh=2
你说没事，可是你看这里，血这么多！
You say it is fine, but look here, there is so much blood!

en=student
"这么多" means "so much."

en=teacher
And then Xiao Ming tries to avoid the doctor.

zh=1
我不打算看医生，我不需要。
I don't plan to see a doctor, I don't need to.

en=student
"我不打算" is "I don't plan to." More "不" negation.

en=teacher
And here is Xiao Hua losing her patience. This is a great example of "both" or "all."

zh=2
你又不听话！我们都得先处理一下这个伤口！
You are being disobedient again! We both need to handle this wound first!

en=student
There it is! "我们都得." "我们" is "we," then "都" is "both/all," and then "得" means "must" or "need to." 

en=teacher
Perfect. "我们都得" means "We both need to." And don't forget our vocab word "伤口," the wound.

en=student
Xiao Ming seems to give in.

zh=1
好吧，好吧。
Alright, alright.

zh=2

Hurry, get a tissue and wipe it.

en=student
And finally, "擦" (cā), the first tone, to wipe.

en=teacher
Exactly. A messy situation, but a great way to practice our negation and our "all" adverb.

That is a great observation. "怎么" is often used to express surprise or to ask for a reason when something unexpected happens. And "流血" means to bleed. "流" means to flow, and "血" is blood.

zh=2

en=teacher
Here is our degree question! "多" (duō) + "疼" (téng). Instead of asking "Are you in pain?" (你疼吗?), she is asking "How painful is it?" or "To what degree are you feeling pain?".

en=student
So it's not "我觉得也," it's "我也觉得."

en=teacher
Correct. If you say "我觉得也," it sounds like you are starting a new thought like "I also think that..." but to say "I also feel," it must be "我也觉得." He also uses "有点" (yǒu diǎn), which means "a little bit," often used for something slightly negative.

Another "多" question! "多大" (duō dà) means "how big" or "how large." We use "大" (big) to ask about size, so "多大" is "how big."

BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's look at that dialogue and break it down line by line. It starts with Manager Wang.

zh=1
当你今天做演示的时候，我真的非常惊讶。
When you were giving your presentation today, I was truly surprised.

en=student
"当你做演示的时候" is our first grammar point. "当...的时候" means "when." Here, "做演示" (zuo yan shi) means to give a presentation or to perform a demonstration.

en=teacher
That is correct. And "惊讶" (jing ya) means surprised. Manager Wang is setting the stage for a critique.

zh=2
啊？为什么，经理？您觉得哪里做得不好吗？
Ah? Why, Manager? Do you think I did something poorly?

en=student
"经理" (jing li) is manager. And "做得不好" (zuo de bu hao) is a common way to say "did something not well" or "did something poorly."

zh=1
虽然你表现得很自信，但会议室里的个个都在看那个错误的数据。
Although you appeared very confident, every single person in the meeting room was looking at that incorrect data.

en=teacher
This is the heart of our grammar lesson. "虽然...但..." (sui ran... dan...) means "although... but...". It's a very important structure for showing contrast.

en=student
And here is the reduplication! "个个" (ge ge). Instead of saying "每个人" (every person), he says "个个," meaning "every single person." It really emphasizes that the mistake was obvious to everyone.

en=teacher
Yes. And "会议室" (hui yi shi) means meeting room. "数据" (shu ju) is data. This is a key professional term.

zh=2
非常抱歉！我准备的时候检查了三次，可能还是漏掉了。
I am so sorry! When I was preparing, I checked three times, but I might have still missed something.

en=student
"准备的时候" (zhun bei de shihou) again, using the "when" structure. "检查" (jian cha) is to check.

en=teacher
And "漏掉了" (lou diao le). "漏" means to leak or to omit, and "掉" is a resultative complement indicating the completion of an action. So, "to miss something" or "to omit something."

zh=1
下次准备的时候，一定要更加仔细一点。
Next time when you are preparing, you must be more careful.

en=student
"下次" (xia ci) is next time. And "仔细" (zi xi) means careful or detailed.

en=teacher
Exactly. "一定要" (yi ding yao) means "must" or "definitely need to."

zh=2
我明白了，谢谢经理。我会重新检查的。
I understand. Thank you, Manager. I will check it again.

en=student
"明白了" (ming bai le) is a great way to say "I understand" or "I've got it." And "重新" (chong xin) means again or anew.

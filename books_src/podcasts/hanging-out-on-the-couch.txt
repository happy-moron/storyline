BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's look at that dialogue, and break it down line by line. It starts with Xiao Li asking a question.

zh=1
你昨天看那个电影了吗？
Did you watch that movie yesterday?

en=student
"你昨天看那个电影了吗?" There is a "了" at the end of that question. Is that related to the past?

en=teacher
Great observation. The "了" here, combined with the context of "昨天" (yesterday), helps indicate that we are talking about a completed action or a specific time in the past. Notice the basic order here: Subject (你), Time (昨天), Verb (看), and Object (那个电影).

zh=2
我没有看电影。我昨天太累了。
I did not watch the movie. I was too tired yesterday.

en=teacher
Here is our key grammar point. Xiao Wang says, "我没有看电影."

en=student
"我没有看电影." So that is the "Subject + 没有 + Verb + Object" structure we talked about?

en=teacher
Exactly. "I" is the subject, "did not watch" is the verb phrase using 没有, and "the movie" is the object. As we mentioned, using 没有 instead of 不 is crucial here because the movie watching was an event that failed to happen in the past.

zh=1
你也没有买东西。我饿了。
You also did not buy anything. I am hungry.

en=student
"你也没有买东西." I noticed the "也" in there. Does it go before the verb?

en=teacher
Yes, it does. "也" (yě), meaning "also" or "too," sits right before the verb. So, "You also did not buy things."

zh=2
我没有买东西。我只是想躺在沙发上。
I did not buy anything. I just want to lie on the couch.

en=student
"我没有买东西." This one is a bit repetitive, but it reinforces the pattern. And then he says "I just want to lie on the couch." "沙发" is the couch, right?

en=teacher
Correct. "沙发" is the couch. And "只是" (zhǐ shì) means "just" or "only."

zh=1
你昨天到底做了什么？
What on earth did you do yesterday?

en=student
"你昨天到底做了什么?" "到底" (dào dǐ) sounds very intense. It is like saying "What on earth" or "What exactly," right?

en=teacher
Precisely. It adds a lot of emphasis to the question, especially when you are frustrated.

zh=2
我什么都没有做。
I did nothing.

en=student
Whoa, this one is interesting. "我什么都没有做." It literally sounds like "I what also have not do."

en=teacher
It is a very common and very useful pattern! When you want to say "nothing" or "not anything," you use the structure "什么 + 都 + Verb." In this case, it is "什么都没有做," which means "did not do anything at all."

zh=1
你还没收拾房间呢！
You have not even tidied the room yet!

en=student
"你还没收拾房间呢!" "还没" (hái méi) sounds like "not yet."

en=teacher
You are on a roll! "还没" means "not yet." And "收拾" (shōushi) means to tidy up or to clean.

zh=2
我只是在睡觉。
I was just sleeping.

en=student
And finally, "I was just sleeping." "睡觉" is to sleep.

en=teacher
Yes. The dialogue shows how these simple structures—negating the past with 没有, using 也 for "also," and using 什么都 for "nothing"—come together to form real, emotive conversation.

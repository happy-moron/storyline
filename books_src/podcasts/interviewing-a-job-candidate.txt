en=teacher
That is a perfect reason to learn today's grammar points. To navigate a stressful interview, you need to explain your reasons clearly and understand the timing of the conversation. Today, we are looking at two very important structures. First, the cause and effect pattern using 因为, which means "because," and 所以, which means "so" or "therefore."

en=student
Right, like "Because it was raining, so I stayed home." You use 因为 for the reason and 所以 for the result. Is it okay to use both? I've heard people sometimes only use one of them.

en=teacher
In very casual conversation, people might drop one, but for the HSK level and for sounding professional, you should use them as a pair. It makes your logic very clear: 因为... 所以...

en=student
That makes sense. And what is the second one?

en=teacher
The second one is expressing that something is "about to happen" using the structure 快, followed by a verb or adjective, and ending with 了. For example, 快下雨了, which means "it is about to rain." It indicates an imminent change in state or an action that will happen very soon.

en=student
Got it. So if the boss is about to walk in, I would say 他快进来了.

en=teacher
Exactly. Now, for today's dialogue, we are heading to a very high-end, very busy restaurant. The atmosphere is tense. You are going to hear a job interview between Mr. Zhao, the restaurant manager, and a candidate named Xiao Wang. Xiao Wang is running a bit late, and the pressure is on.

BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's break down that intense conversation. It starts with the manager, Mr. Zhao.

zh=1
你怎么才来？我们快要开始面试了！
Why are you just arriving? We are about to start the interview!

en=student
"你怎么才来?" That "才" (cái) is interesting. It's not just "how," it adds the feeling that something happened later than expected, like "you're *just* now arriving."

en=teacher
Spot on. And then he says "我们快要开始面试了" (wǒ men kuài yào kāi shǐ miàn shì le). This is our second grammar point. "快要...了" indicates the interview is about to begin.

en=student
And Xiao Wang responds with an apology.

zh=2
对不起！因为路上的交通太拥挤了，所以我迟到了。
I am sorry! Because the traffic on the road was too crowded, so I was late.

en=teacher
Here is our first grammar point in action. "因为路上的交通太拥挤了" (yīn wèi lù shàng de jiāo tōng tài yōng jǐ le) is the reason. The reason is that the traffic was too crowded.

en=student
"交通" (jiāo tōng) is traffic, and "拥挤" (yōng jǐ) is crowded. And then he uses "所以" (suǒ yǐ) to give the result: "所以我迟到了" (suǒ yǐ wǒ chí dào le), "so I was late."

en=teacher
Exactly. "迟到" (chí dào) is a very important verb meaning to be late. Now, Mr. Zhao isn't making it easy for him.

zh=1
因为你迟到了，所以我对你的印象不太好。
Because you are late, my impression of you is not very good.

en=student
Ouch. He's being very blunt. "印象" (yìn xiàng) is impression. So, "my impression of you is not very good."

en=teacher
It's a tough environment! Xiao Wang tries to defend himself here.

zh=2
我明白。但我非常热爱餐饮业，我也准备了很久，所以我很想在这里工作。
I understand. But I love the catering industry very much, and I have also prepared for a long time, so I really want to work here.

en=student
He's using both "热爱" (rè ài), which is a strong way to say "to love" or "to be passionate about," and the "因为...所以..." structure again. But I noticed he used "我也" (wǒ yě) in there.

en=teacher
Good ear. "我也" means "I also." He is adding another reason for his passion. And he says "准备了很久" (zhǔn bèi le hěn jiǔ), meaning "prepared for a long time." The "了" here indicates the completion of the preparation.

en=student
Then the manager cuts him off.

zh=1
好吧。因为客人快要到了，所以我们得快点结束面试。
Alright. Because the guests are about to arrive, we must end the interview quickly.

en=teacher
"客人快要到了" (kè rén kuài yào dào le). The guests are about to arrive. This is that "imminent action" again. And because of that, he uses "所以" to say the interview must end quickly.

en=student
"得" (děi) means "must" or "have to," right? So, "we must end the interview quickly."

en=teacher
Correct. "快点" (kuài diǎn) means "a bit faster" or "quickly."

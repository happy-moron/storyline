Well, that is perfect, because today we will learn how to give instructions and ask about dimensions and degrees. First, we have the negative command using 别. You place 别 before a verb to tell someone "don't" do something. For example, 别走 (don't leave) or 别说话 (don't talk). It is a direct way to give an instruction or a warning.

en=student
So, if I am painting and I am being too messy, I could say 别乱 (don't be messy)? Or is it 别弄乱 (don't make it messy)?

en=teacher
Exactly! 别弄乱 is a perfect use of the structure. Second, we are looking at asking about degree using 多. When you place 多 before an adjective, it turns a statement into a question about "how [adjective]." For example, 你多大? (How old are you?) or 你多高? (How tall are you?). In our lesson, we will use it to ask about the size of our brushstrokes and the depth of our colors.

en=student
Got it. So, instead of saying "the color is very deep," I can ask "how deep is the color?" using 多.

en=teacher
Precisely. Before we listen to our dialogue, let's look at our key vocabulary. 

en=teacher

en=teacher
We have 画笔, (fourth tone and third tone), meaning paintbrush... 画笔。

en=teacher
We have 颜色, (second tone and fourth tone), meaning color... 颜色。

en=teacher
We have 漂亮, (fourth tone and light tone), meaning pretty... 漂亮。

en=teacher
We have 乱, (fourth tone), meaning messy... 乱。

zh=1

zh=2

BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's listen to that dialogue again and break it down. We start with the teacher's command.

zh=1
别动！你的手别动。
Don't move! Don't move your hand.

en=student
"别动" (bié dòng). "别" is "don't" and "动" means "to move." It is such a common command.

en=teacher
Right. Then the student asks a question using our degree grammar.

zh=2
对不起，老师。这个树要画多大？
I am sorry, teacher. How big should this tree be?

en=student
"这个树要画多大？" That's the "Subj. + 多 + Adj. ?" structure. "多大" (duō dà) means "how big." "要" (yào) here means "should."

en=teacher
Correct. Now the teacher gives more instructions.

zh=1
别画太大了。颜色也要注意。别用太多水。
Don't paint it too big. Pay attention to the color too. Don't use too much water.

en=student
"别画太大了." We used "别" (don't) and "画" (to paint), but then we added "太大了" (too big). So, "don't paint it too big." 

en=teacher
Yes. And notice "颜色" (yán sè), color. "也要注意" (yě yào zhù yì) means "also need to pay attention." Then, "别用太多水" (bié yòng tài duō shuǐ) means "don't use too much water."

en=student
"用" (yòng) is "to use." It is a very versatile verb.

en=teacher
It is. Now, the student gets excited.

zh=2
我知道了。老师，你看这朵花，多漂亮啊！
I understand. Teacher, look at this flower, how pretty it is!

en=student
"多漂亮啊!" Here, the student is using "多" in an exclamation. It's similar to the question form, but it means "how [adjective]!" Like, "How pretty!"

en=teacher
Exactly. It's a way to express emphasis. Now the teacher critiques the technique.

zh=1
还可以。但是你的画笔有点脏。别乱画。
Not bad. But your paintbrush is a little dirty. Don't paint messily.

en=student
"还可以" (hái kě yǐ) is a classic way to say "it's okay" or "not bad." And "画笔" (huà bǐ) is the paintbrush. "脏" (zāng) is dirty.

en=teacher
Then, "别乱画" (bié luàn huà). This is a great combination. "乱" means messy, and "画" is to paint. So, "don't paint messily."

en=student
The student asks for a final opinion.

zh=2
老师，您觉得这幅画多好？
Teacher, how good do you think this painting is?

en=teacher
"您觉得" (nín jué de) is the polite way to say "you think." And "多好" (duō hǎo) is our "how [adjective]" structure. "How good?"

en=student
And the teacher gives one last piece of advice.

zh=1
还行，别太骄傲。
It is okay, don't be too proud.

en=teacher
"还行" (hái xíng) is another way to say "it's alright." And "别太骄傲" (bié tài jiāo ào) means "don't be too proud." "骄傲" (jiāo ào) is a great word for pride or arrogance.

That is a perfect segue, Ryan. Today, we are focusing on two fundamental verbs. First, the verb 叫, which means "to be called" or "to call." It is the most common way to introduce yourself or someone else by name. For example, 我叫 Ryan (I am called Ryan) or 他叫小明 (He is called Xiao Ming).

en=student

Exactly. It is much more natural in spoken Chinese than using the verb "to be." Second, we have the verb 去, which means "to go." This is used when you are moving from your current location to a destination. The structure is very simple: 去 followed by the place. For example, 我去学校 (I go to school) or 我们去餐厅 (We are going to a restaurant).

Welcome to Learning Chinese. I am Ellen, and today we are diving into the festive and delicious world of the Mid-Autumn Festival.

en=student
I love that festival! The mooncakes are amazing, but the preparation can be pretty chaotic.

en=teacher
It really can be. And because it is a time of big celebrations, it is perfect for practicing two very useful grammar points. First, we are looking at how to emphasize quantity or collective actions using the word 都. You use this after a subject like "everyone" or "many people" to say that "all" or "both" of them are doing something. For example, 大家都很忙, which means everyone is very busy.

en=student
So, if I want to say "We are all students," I would say 我们都是学生?

en=teacher
Exactly. You place it right before the verb or adjective. Second, we are learning how to express that something is "about to" happen using the structure 还有...就要...了. This is used when you want to mention a specific time period remaining before an event occurs. For example, 我们还有三天就要考试了, which means we have three days left until the exam.

en=student
So it's like saying, "In [amount of time], [something] is about to happen."

en=teacher
Precisely. It adds a sense of anticipation or urgency. Before we hear the dialogue, let's look at our six key vocabulary words. 

en=teacher
First, we have 月饼, first tone, third tone, meaning mooncake... 月饼.

en=teacher
Second, 团圆, second tone, second tone, meaning to reunite or reunion... 团圆.

en=teacher
Third, 准备, third tone, fourth tone, meaning to prepare... 准备.

en=teacher
Fourth, 忙, second tone, meaning busy... 忙.

en=teacher
Fifth, 礼物, third tone, fourth tone, meaning gift... 礼物.

en=teacher
And sixth, 节日, second tone, second tone, meaning festival... 节日.

en=student
Got it. Mooncakes, reunions, preparing, being busy, gifts, and festivals.

BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's look at that dialogue and break it down line by line. We start with Li Hua, who sounds quite stressed.

zh=1
哎呀，我真忙！中秋节马上就要到了。
Oh, I am so busy! The Mid-Autumn Festival is almost here.

en=student
"哎呀" is like "oh boy" or "oh dear," right? It really captures her frustration. And she says "中秋节马上就要到了." We just learned "就要...了"!

en=teacher
Yes! "就要...了" indicates something is about to happen. Adding "马上" before it just makes it feel even more imminent, like "is about to happen right now."

zh=2
别担心，我们还有三天就要开始准备庆祝活动了。
Don't worry, we still have three days before we start preparing for the celebrations.

en=student
There it is! "还有三天就要...了." So, "three days left until we start preparing." 

en=teacher
Correct. The structure is "还有" plus the time duration, then "就要" plus the action. It's very common when looking at a calendar.

zh=1
你说得倒容易。现在大家都很忙，超市里的人很多。
Easy for you to say. Everyone is so busy right now, and there are so many people in the supermarket.

en=student
"你说得倒容易" is such a natural way to say "Easy for you to say." And then she uses our first grammar point: "大家都很忙."

en=teacher
Exactly. "大家" means everyone, and "都" emphasizes that it's not just one person, but every single person in that group who is busy. 

en=student
Wait, what about "很多人都去买月饼了" later in the dialogue? Is that the same rule?

en=teacher
It's the same principle. "很多人" acts as the subject meaning "many people," and "都" tells us that all those many people are doing the action of buying mooncakes. Without "都," the sentence still works, but "都" adds that emphasis of "all of them."

zh=2
确实，很多人都去买月饼了。
It is true, many people have already gone to buy mooncakes.

en=student
"确实" means "indeed" or "true." It's a great way to agree with someone.

zh=1
没错，我也得赶紧去买一些礼物。
Exactly, I also need to hurry and buy some gifts.

en=student
"礼物" is gift. She's saying she needs to hurry and buy some.

en=teacher
And "赶紧" means "hurriedly" or "quickly." It adds to her sense of being rushed.

zh=2
别急，我们还要准备很多好吃的呢。
Don't worry, we also have a lot of delicious food to prepare.

en=student
"别急" means "don't worry" or "don't be in such a rush." 

en=teacher
And we end with "准备," our vocabulary word for "to prepare." She is trying to calm Li Hua down by reminding her that there is still plenty of delicious food to get ready for the big reunion.

en=student
The Mid-Autumn Festival really does involve a lot of "准备" and "忙" for everyone!

en=teacher
It certainly does. It's all about the "团圆" at the end of all that work.

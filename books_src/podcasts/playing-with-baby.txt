Indeed! But even when we are playing, there are specific structures we need to master. Today, we are focusing on two essential HSK grammar points. First, the placement of time words. In Chinese, time words are very flexible. You can place them before the subject or after the subject, as long as they come before the verb. For example, 我明天去 (I'm going tomorrow) or 明天我去 (Tomorrow I am going). Both are correct.

en=student
So, "Time + Subject" or "Subject + Time"? It's like having two different ways to say the same thing.

Correct. To tell someone "Don't" do something, you simply put 别 before the verb. For example, 别走 (Don't leave) or 别说话 (Don't talk). In our dialogue, you will hear it used when the customer is feeling quite frustrated.

BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's look at that dialogue, and break it down line by line. It starts with the customer.

zh=1
我要退掉这台手机。
I want to return this phone.

en=student
"我要退掉这台手机。" "要" means "to want." "退掉" is interesting. "退" means to return, but what about "掉"?

en=teacher
Good observation. In Chinese, we often use "掉" after a verb to indicate that the action is completed or that something has been removed or dealt with. So, "退掉" means to complete the return process. And "手机" is the standard word for a mobile phone.

zh=2
让我看看。问题有多大？
Let me see. How big is the problem?

en=student
"问题有多大？" We see our grammar point here! "问题" means problem, and "有多大" means "how big." 

en=teacher
Right. Remember, "多" plus an adjective asks for the degree. Here, the clerk is asking to what extent the problem is large.

zh=1
非常大！你看这个屏幕！
It's very big! Look at this screen!

en=student
"非常大" means very big. And "屏幕" is the screen of a device, like a phone or a computer.

zh=2
哦，只是一个小划痕。
Oh, it's just a small scratch.

en=student
"划痕" is a great word. It means scratch. It sounds like a very specific technical term for a surface being marked.

en=teacher
It is. It comes from the verb "划" which means to scratch or to draw a line.

zh=1
别骗我！这个裂缝非常大！
Don't lie to me! This crack is very big!

en=student
And there it is! "别骗我!" "别" is the "don't," and "骗" means to lie or to cheat.

en=teacher
Yes. "别" is very common for commands. Now, look at the next sentence: "这个裂缝非常大。" "裂缝" is a different word than "划痕." 

en=student
Right, "划痕" is a scratch, but "裂缝" is a crack, right? Like a deep break?

en=teacher
Exactly. A scratch is a surface mark, but a crack is a structural break.

zh=2
请别生气。我给您换一个。
Please don't be angry. I will exchange it for you.

en=student
"请别生气." "别" again for the negative command, and "生气" means to get angry. It's a very high-frequency word.

en=teacher
It is. Also, notice she says "我给您换一个." "换" means to change or to exchange. She is offering an exchange.

zh=1
别耽误时间了，我现在就要换。
Don't waste time, I want to exchange it right now.

en=student
"别耽误时间了." "耽误" is a heavy word. It means to delay, to waste, or to hold up.

en=teacher
Yes, "耽误时间" is the standard way to say "to waste time" or "to cause a delay." The customer is being very impatient!

zh=2
好的，请等一下。
Okay, please wait a moment.

en=student
"等一下" is "wait a moment." A classic phrase for service workers.

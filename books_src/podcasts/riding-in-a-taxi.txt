BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's break down this stressful taxi ride line by line. We start with the passenger.

zh=1
师傅，请带我去机场。今天路怎么样？
Driver, please take me to the airport. How is the road today?

en=student
"师傅" (shī fu) is a very common way to address a driver or a skilled worker, like a master. And then we see our first grammar point: "今天路怎么样？".

en=teacher
Right. "路" (lù) means road. "怎么样" (zěn me yàng) asks about the condition of the road. It's not asking "how do I walk the road," but rather "how is the state of the road?"

zh=2
今天的路不太好。
The road today is not very good.

en=student
"不太好" (bú tài hǎo) means not very good. It's a classic way to soften a negative statement.

zh=1
为什么？现在不是不堵车吗？
Why? Isn't there no traffic right now?

en=teacher
Here we see "为什么" (wèi shén me), which means "why." It's placed right where the reason would be. And "堵车" (dǔ chē) means a traffic jam or "cars being blocked."

zh=2
现在是下班时间，路上面有很多车。
It is rush hour right now, there are many cars on the road.

en=student
"下班时间" (xià bān shí jiān). "下班" means to finish work, and "时间" means time. So, "the time after finishing work," or rush hour.

zh=1
我有一个很重要的会议。我们什么时候能到？
I have a very important meeting. When can we arrive?

en=student
"很重要的会议" (hěn zhòng yào de huì yì). "重要" means important, and "会议" is meeting. Then, "我们什么时候能到？" This is the perfect example of question word placement. We want to ask "When," so we put "什么时候" (shén me shí hou) exactly where the time answer would go.

en=teacher
Exactly. You wouldn't say "When can we arrive?" in the same way in English. In Chinese, it's "We when can arrive?" 

zh=2
十五分钟以后。哎，哎，你看，前面那辆车怎么了？
In fifteen minutes. Hey, hey, look, what happened to that car in front?

en=student
"十五分钟" (shí wǔ fēn zhōng) is fifteen minutes. And then the driver says "怎么了？" (zěn me le). This is a very common phrase.

en=teacher
Yes! "怎么了" is used to ask "what happened?" or "what's wrong?" when you notice something unusual.

zh=1
那是什么车？为什么停在路中间？
What car is that? Why is it parked in the middle of the road?

en=student
"那是什么车？" (nà shì shén me chē). Again, the question word "什么" (shén me) is placed right before the noun "车" (chē). It's "That is what car?"

en=teacher
And "停在路中间" (tíng zài lù zhōng jiān). "停" is to stop or park, and "中间" means middle.

zh=2
我不知道，可能是出车祸了。
I don't know, maybe there was a car accident.

en=student
"车祸" (chē huò). "车" is car, and "祸" is disaster or accident. So, a car accident.

en=teacher
It's a bit of a tense ending to the dialogue! But it really shows how to use "why," "when," and "what" in a natural setting.

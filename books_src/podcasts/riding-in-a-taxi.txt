"很堵" is a great one for travelers—it means "very congested" or "heavy traffic." But I noticed something. He used 会 to ask if she can drive.

en=teacher
Yes! This is a perfect example of 会 expressing a learned skill. He isn't asking if she has the physical strength to drive, but if she possesses the skill of driving.

zh=2

en=student
She uses "不会" to say she doesn't have that skill. And she uses "只想" to say she "only wants to."

en=student
"没关系" is the classic "it doesn't matter" or "no problem." He says "我知道" for "I know." And he uses "很快" for "very fast."

en=teacher
And notice how he uses 会 again at the end of that sentence: "不会迷路的." Here, he is using 会 to express a future certainty or a prediction—that they will not get lost. It's like saying, "It won't happen."

zh=2
那就好。我有点担心会迷路，我怕会迟到。
That is good. I am a little worried about getting lost; I am afraid I will be late.

en=student
This line is quite packed! She says "有点担心," which means "a little worried." Then she says "会迷路" and "会迟到."

en=teacher
Yes, in both cases, she is expressing a possibility or a likelihood of something happening in the future. "I might get lost" or "It's likely I'll be late."

en=student

"别担心" is "don't worry." And "放心" is our other key word, "to rest assured."

en=teacher

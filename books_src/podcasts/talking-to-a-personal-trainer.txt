en=student

en=teacher
Well, today’s lesson will help you express exactly that! We are focusing on two very important grammar points. First, how to turn an adjective into an adverb using the particle 地. You place 地 between an adjective and a verb to describe how an action is performed. For example, 慢慢地走, which means to walk slowly, or 认真地学习, which means to study seriously.

en=student
So, the adjective describes the manner of the action. It is like adding "ly" to a word in English.

en=teacher
Exactly. Now, the second point is how to express that something has happened again using 又 and 了. While the word 又 means "again," we use the structure 又 + Verb + 了 to indicate that a repeated action has already occurred. For example, 他又来了, which means he came again.

en=student
Wait, I know "again" can also be said with 再. What is the difference?

en=teacher
That is a great question. Use 再 for things that *will* happen again in the future, like 再见, which means "see you again." But use 又 for things that *have already* happened. If you did something again yesterday, you use 又.

en=student
That makes sense. It’s about whether the repetition is in the past or the future.

en=teacher
Precisely. In today's dialogue, you are going to hear a very intense session between a personal trainer and a client who is reaching his absolute limit. The trainer is being quite demanding, and the client is definitely feeling the struggle. Pay attention to how they describe the intensity of the movements and the repetition of the exercises.

BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Alright, let's break down that intense workout. We start with the trainer.

zh=1
别停！快快地挥动手臂！
Don't stop! Swing your arms quickly!

en=student
"别停" means "don't stop." Then we have "快快地挥动手臂." We see our first grammar point here: 快快地. The adjective 快, which means fast, is doubled to 快快 to add emphasis, and then we add 地 to turn it into an adverb. "挥动" means to swing or wave, and "手臂" means arms.

en=teacher
That's right. Now, let's hear the client, who sounds like he's about to collapse.

zh=2
我不行了！我又做了十次了！
I can't do it! I've done this ten times again!

en=student
"我不行了" is a very common way to say "I can't do it anymore" or "I'm done." Then we have "我又做了十次了." Here is our second grammar point: 又...了. The client is saying he has *already* done the action ten times again.

en=teacher
Right. The "了" at the end emphasizes that the action is completed.

zh=1
别抱怨！稳稳地呼吸！
Don't complain! Breathe steadily!

en=student
"抱怨" means to complain. Then "稳稳地呼吸." We have the adjective 稳, meaning steady, doubled to 稳稳, followed by 地 to describe how he should breathe. And "呼吸" is the verb, to breathe.

en=teacher
Let's listen to the client's frustration.

zh=2
你太狠了！你又让我练这个了！
You are too harsh! You are making me do this again!

en=student
"你太狠了" is a great phrase. 狠 means harsh or ruthless. But look at the grammar here: "你又让我练这个了." We have "又" and "了" again. This time, the verb being repeated is "让," which means "to make" or "to let" someone do something. So, "you are making me do this again."

en=teacher
Very good. The trainer gives one last push.

zh=1
坚持住！努力地练！
Hold on! Train hard!

en=student
"坚持住" means to hold on or persevere. And "努力地练." "努力" means hard-working or diligent, followed by 地, describing the manner of the training. "练" is a short version of 练习, to practice or train.

en=teacher
And finally, the exhausted client.

zh=2
我慢吞吞地走着……
I am walking sluggishly...

en=student
"慢吞吞地" is a wonderful way to say "sluggishly" or "slowly." The word 慢 means slow, and adding 吞吞 makes it much more descriptive of that sluggish, heavy feeling.

en=teacher
It really captures the exhaustion.

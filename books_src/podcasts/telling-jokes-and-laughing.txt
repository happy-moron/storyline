I hope my jokes are better than my Chinese grammar, Ellen. Speaking of time, I always get confused when the minutes get complicated. Is it just saying the number and then adding a word for minutes?

en=teacher
Exactly! In Chinese, telling the time is quite straightforward. For the hour, we use 点, which is diǎn. For the minutes, we use 分, which is fēn. So, if it is eight o'clock, you say 八点. If it is eight ten, you say 八点十分.

en=student
Okay, so it's just "number plus point plus number plus minute." But what about two o'clock? Do I say 二点?

en=teacher
That is a very common mistake! When we are telling time, for the number two, we do not use 二. We use 两, which is liǎng. So, two o'clock is 两点. If you say 二点, people will understand you, but it sounds very unnatural. 

en=student

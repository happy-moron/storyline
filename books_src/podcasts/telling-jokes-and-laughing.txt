INTRO

en=teacher
Welcome to Learning Chinese. I am Ellen, and today we are going to talk about something that can be quite awkward if you don't know the right words: jokes, laughter, and telling time.

en=student
That sounds like a fun topic, Ellen. I love telling jokes, but I'm not sure if my Chinese jokes are actually funny or if people are just being polite.

en=teacher
Exactly! We need to learn how to express if something is actually funny or if it is not funny at all. Today, we are focusing on two key grammar points regarding time. First, the basic way to say the hour, which is using the word 点, pronounced diǎn, meaning "o'clock." For example, 三点, which is three o'clock. 

en=student
And the more advanced version?

en=teacher
When you want to include the minutes, you use the structure: Number plus 点 plus Number plus 分. The word 分, pronounced fēn, means "minutes." For example, 三点十分, which is three ten, or 三点三十分, which is three thirty. You can also add the word 钟, pronounced zhōng, meaning "clock," after the hour, like 三点钟, to emphasize it is exactly that hour, but it is optional.

en=student
Okay, so if it is ten thirty, I say 十点三十分. And if it is just ten o'clock, I just say 十点.

en=teacher
Perfect. Now, let's look at our six key vocabulary words for today. 

en=teacher
First, we have 笑, (fourth tone), meaning to laugh or to smile. 笑.

en=teacher
Next, 笑话, (fourth tone and fourth tone), which means a joke. 笑话.

en=teacher
Then, we have 好笑, (third tone and fourth tone), meaning funny or laughable. 好笑.

en=teacher
Next is 有趣, (third tone and fourth tone), which means interesting or amusing. 有趣.

en=teacher
Fifth, we have 一点也不, (first tone, third tone, third tone, fourth tone), which means "not at all." 一点也不.

en=teacher
And finally, 时间, (second tone and first tone), which means time. 时间.

en=student
Got it. So we can talk about whether a joke is funny and exactly what time we should go out to eat.

en=teacher
Exactly. In today's dialogue, you are going to hear two friends, Dawei and Li Hua. Dawei is trying very hard to be the life of the party by telling a joke, but Li Hua is... well, she is not easily amused. They will also check the time to decide when to head out.

BREAKDOWN

en=teacher
Let's look at that dialogue and break it down line by line. We start with Dawei.

zh=1
你听过这个笑话吗？很有趣！
Have you heard this joke? It is very interesting!

en=student
"你听过这个笑话吗？" He is asking if you have heard it. "听过" means "to have heard" because of that "guo" particle. And "笑话" is our word for joke.

en=teacher
Right. And he describes it as "很有趣". Remember, 有趣 means interesting or amusing.

en=student
Then Li Hua responds, "我没听过。快点说吧。" She sounds a bit impatient!

en=teacher
She is! "快点" means "a bit faster" or "hurry up."

zh=1
一个学生为什么带梯子去学校？因为他想去高中！
Why did a student bring a ladder to school? Because he wanted to go to high school!

en=student
That is a classic bad joke! "为什么" is why, "带" is to bring, and "梯子" is a ladder. And "高中" means high school.

en=teacher
And then comes the reaction. Li Hua says:

zh=2
这一点也不好笑。
This is not funny at all.

en=student
"这一点也不好笑." We saw "一点也不" in our vocab list. It means "not at all." And "好笑" is funny. So she's saying it's not funny at all.

en=teacher
Exactly. Now Dawei tries to lighten the mood, but he uses our time grammar.

zh=1
别这样嘛！现在都十点三十分了。
Don't be like that! It is already ten thirty!

en=student
"十点三十分" is ten thirty. We use "点" for the hour and "分" for the minutes. Is it necessary to say "钟" here?

en=teacher
Not at all. In casual speech, we usually just say "点" and "分." If you say "十点三十分钟," it sounds a bit repetitive.

zh=2
我没心情笑。现在是十点三十分，我饿了。
I am not in the mood to laugh. It is ten thirty, and I am hungry.

en=student
"我没心情" is a great phrase. It means "I am not in the mood." And again, "十点三十分." It's very consistent.

en=teacher
It is. Then they move to the next part of the conversation.

zh=1
好吧，那我们去吃饭吧。现在几点？
Fine, let's go eat then. What time is it now?

en=student
"几点" is the standard way to ask "what time."

zh=2
现在是十点三十五分。
It is ten thirty-five.

en=teacher
And there is our advanced time structure: "十点三十五分." Number, dot, number, minute.

zh=1
走吧！
Let's go!

en=student
"走吧" is such a useful phrase for "let's go."


== Parts of Speech ==
These following grammar points fit pretty neatly into one part of speech category.

=== Adverbs ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Expressing actions in progress with "zai"
|(正) 在 + Verb
|我们 正在 吃饭。
We are eating rice now.
|-
|Negation of "you" with "mei"
|没 + 有
|我 没有 钱 。
I don't have money.
|-
|Standard negation with "bu"
|不 + Verb / Adj.
|他们 不 是 坏 孩子。
They are not bad children.
|-
|The "all" adverb "dou"
|都 + Verb / Adj.
|我们 都 住 在 上海。
We all live in Shanghai.
|-
|The "also" adverb "ye"
|也 + Verb / Adj.
|昨天 很 冷，今天 也 '''很''' 冷。
Yesterday was cold, and today is also cold.
|}

=== Adverbs with Adjectives ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Asking about degree with "duo"
|Subj. + 多 + Adj. ?
|他 多 高？
How tall is he?
|-
|Expressing "not very" with "bu tai"
|不太 + Adj.
|他 不太 高兴 。我 不太 喜欢。
He isn't very happy. I don't quite like it.
|}

=== Conjunctions ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)

!Examples

|我 和 他 都 不 去。
I and him didn't go.
|}

=== Nouns ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|After a specific time with "yihou"
|Time / Verb + 以后
|10点 以后 ，我 不 在 家。
I won't be at home after 10 o' clock.
|-
|Before a specific time with "yiqian"
|Time / Verb + 以前
|十 点 以前，我 在 家。
I will be at home before 10 o' clock.
|-
|Time words and word order
|Subj. + Time⋯⋯ / Time + Subj.⋯⋯
|我 明天 有 空。/ 明天 我 有 空。
I will be free tomorrow.
|}

=== Numbers ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Age with "sui"
|Subj. + Number + 岁
|我 20 岁 。
I'm 20 years old.
|-
|Measure word "ge"
|Number + 个 + Noun
|一 个 人 。
A person.
|-
|Structure of dates
|Number + 年 + Number + 月 + Number + 日
|今天 是 2011年 11月 11日/号 。
Today is 11th November 2011.
|-
|Structure of days of the week
|星期 + Number
|今天 星期三 。
Today is Wednesday.
|-
|Structure of numbers
|Number + Unit
|三 百 五 十 九。
359.
|-
|Structure of times (advanced)
|Number 1 + 点(钟) + Number 2 + 分
|现在 十一点十八分 。
It's 11:18 now.
|-
|Structure of times (basic)
|Number + 点(钟)
|现在 十一点 。
It's 11 o' clock now.
|}

=== Particles ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Expressing close possession without "de"
|Pronoun + Noun
|我 女朋友
my girlfriend
|-
|Expressing completion with "le"
|Subj. + Verb + 了 + Obj.
|我 吃 了 两 个 苹果。
I ate 2 apples.
|-
|Expressing "not anymore" with "le"
|不 / 没(有) + Verb Phrase + 了
|我 不 想 吃 了 。
I don't want to eat anymore.
|-
|Expressing "now" with "le"
|New Situation + 了
|吃饭 了！
Time to eat!
|-
|Expressing possession with "de"
|Noun 1 + 的 + Noun 2
|我 的 老师
my teacher
|-
|Questions with "ne"
|⋯⋯呢 ?
|我 很 好。你 呢 ?
I'm fine. And you?
|-
|Sentence-final interjection "a"
|⋯⋯啊
|是 啊！谁 啊？
Yep! Who?
|-
|Softening speech with "ba"
|⋯⋯吧
|这样 不 太 好 吧。
This ain't that good.
|-
|Suggestions with "ba"
|Command + 吧
|我们 走 吧 。
Let's go.
|}

=== Verbs ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Directional verbs "lai" and "qu"
|来 / 去 + Place
|我 来 上海 一 年 了。
It's been 1 year I came to Shanghai.
|-
|Expressing existence in a place with "zai"
|在 + Place
|老师 在 上海 。
The teacher is at Shanghai.
|-
|Expressing existence with "you"
|Place + 有 + Obj.
|我们 学校 有 很 多 学生。
Our school has lots of students.
|-
|Expressing possession with "you"
|Subj. + 有 + Obj.
|我 有 钱。
I have money.
|-
|Polite requests with "qing"
|请 + Verb
|请 坐 。
Please sit.
|-
|Using the verb "jiao"
|叫 + Name
|你 叫 什么 名字 ？
What's your name?
|-
|Using the verb "qu"
|去 + Place
|我 去 学校。
I go to school.
|}

==== Auxiliary verbs ====
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Expressing ability or possibility with "neng"
|能 + Verb
|中文 这么 难，你 能 学 好 吗？
Chinese is tough, can you master it?
|-
|Expressing a learned skill with "hui"
|会 + Verb
|他 会 说 中文 。
He can speak Chinese.
|-
|Expressing "will" with "hui"
|会 + Verb
|明天 你 会 来 吗？
Are you going to come tomorrow?
|-
|Expressing "would like to" with "xiang"
|想 + Verb
|我 想 去。
I want to go.
|}

==== Verb phrases ====
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples

== Grammatical Structures ==
These are grammatical structures that do not correspond to one particular part of speech.

=== Basics ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Basic sentence order
|Subj. + Verb (+ Obj.)
|我 爱 你 。
I love you.
|-
|Connecting nouns with "shi"
|A + 是 + B
|我 是 学生 。
I'm a student.
|-
|Expressing "excessively" with "tai"
|太 + Adj. + 了
|太 好 了 !
That's great!
|-
|Simple "noun + adjective" sentences
|Noun + 很 + Adj.
|我 很 好。
I'm fine.
|}

=== Numbers and Measure Words ===

!Examples

=== Question Forms ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Asking how something is with "zenmeyang"
|⋯⋯怎么样？
|你 最近 怎么样 ？
How are you recently?
|-
|Placement of question words
|什么 / 什么时候 / 谁 / 哪儿 / 为什么 / 怎么
|你 是 谁 ？你 在 哪儿 ？
Who are you? Where are you?
|-
|Questions with "le ma"
|Verb + 了 + 吗？
|你 吃饭 了 吗 ？
Have you ate yet?
|-
|Yes-no questions with "ma"
|⋯⋯吗？
|他 是 老板 吗 ?
Is he a boss?
|}

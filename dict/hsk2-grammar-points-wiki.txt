=== Adverbs ===
{| class="wikitable"
!Grammar Point (English)
!Pattern
!Examples
|-
|Continuation with "hai"
|Subj. + 还 + Verb Phrase / Adj.
|你 还在 看 书 ？
You are still reading?
|-
|Emphasis with "jiushi"
|就是 + Verb
|我 父母 就是 不 让 我 一个人 去 。
My parents wouldn't let me go alone.
|-
|Emphasizing quantity with "dou"
|大家 / 很多人 + 都⋯⋯
|很 多 地方 都 有 wifi。
There are internet in lots of places.
|-
|Expressing "about to" with "jiuyao"
|还有 + Time Period + 就要 + Verb Phrase + 了
|还有 五 天 就要 放假 了 。
Holidays are going to start in 5 more days.
|-
|Expressing "again" in the future with "zai"
|再 + Verb
|我 下 次 再 来。
I'll come next time.
|-
|Expressing "already" with "dou"
|都 + Time + 了
|都 九点 '''了''' ，快点 起床 ！
It's already 9 o'clock, quickly wake up!
|-
|Expressing "already" with "yijing"
|已经⋯⋯了
|他 已经 走 了 。
He left.
|-
|Expressing "and also" with "hai"
|还 + Verb
|她 有 一个 弟弟，还 有 一个 妹妹。
She has a younger brother and a younger sister.
|-
|Expressing earliness with "jiu"
|Subj. + Time + 就 + Verb + 了
|我 昨晚 八 点 半 就 睡觉 '''了''' 。
I slept at half past 8 yesterday.
|-
|Expressing "then" with "jiu"
|就 + Verb Phrase
|我们 现在 有 时间，就 去吧。
We have the time now, so let's go.
|-
|Negative commands with "bie"
|别 + Verb
|别走。
Don't leave.
|}

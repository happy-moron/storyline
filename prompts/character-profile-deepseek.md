Example 1 — Reluctant Sci-Fi Hero

Project / Genre: The Last Cargo — grounded sci-fi adventure/drama
Character: Marek Voss
Age / Gender / Vocal age: 34 / male / late 30s
Role / Archetype: Lead — cynical smuggler with a buried heart
Tone of project: Gritty but warm, dry humor
VOICE SNAPSHOT

    Pitch: Medium-low

    Texture: Gravelly, warm, slightly frayed; smoker’s rasp without heavy damage

    Tempo / rhythm: Relaxed and measured; slows when lying or buying time

    Accent / diction: Neutral American with a faint outer-rim drawl; lazy consonants

    Breath / placement: Chest voice, low breath support, often speaks on an exhale

    Signature vocal mannerism: Dry chuckle before deflecting; sighs into sarcasm

PERSONALITY

    Core traits: Sardonic, self-reliant, secretly principled, emotionally guarded

    External want: Pay off debt, keep the ship flying, avoid attachments

    Internal fear / need: Fear of being responsible for people he can’t protect

    Public mask vs. private self: Public — unbothered wiseass. Private — hypervigilant and caring

SPEECH & LANGUAGE

    Vocabulary / syntax: Colloquial, short sentences, ship jargon, deadpan understatement

    Verbal tics / filler: “Look…”, “Kid,” “Ain’t,” “Yeah, no.”

    Use of silence / interruption: Uses pauses; lets silence do the work; avoids heart-to-hearts

HOW THEY SOUND IN DIFFERENT SCENARIOS

    Casual / relaxed: Easy, low, smile in the voice; jokes land dry

    Happy / excited: Doesn’t get big; tempo rises, more clipped, ironic cheer

    Angry / threatened: Drops colder and quieter; menace through control, not shouting

    Sad / vulnerable: Loses gravel, more breathy, slower, swallows words

    Scared / lying: Slightly faster, over-casual, overly specific; then catches himself and slows

    Intimate / sincere: Low, close-mic softness, steady; no sarcasm, simple words

KEY RELATIONSHIPS & VOCAL SHIFTS

    Jessa — young stowaway: softer, warmer, patient; calls her “kid”

    Renn — old rival: tighter, clipped, faster competitive banter

    Delacroix — creditor: polite, flat, coiled; overly formal, dangerous calm

BACKSTORY (1–2 LINES)

Former colonial pilot who lost his crew in a botched evacuation. Now runs small cargo and refuses to lead.
AUDITION DIRECTION

    Lean into: Dry humor, understated danger, warmth that slips out accidentally

    Avoid: Full-on tough-guy cliché, cartoon pirate, or sentimental monologues

Example 2 — Elegant Fantasy Antagonist

Project / Genre: The Orrery of Ash — dark fantasy / psychological horror
Character: Vespera Lune
Age / Gender / Vocal age: Appears 40 / female / ageless
Role / Archetype: Antagonist — manipulative oracle
Tone of project: Elegant, unsettling, slow-burn menace
VOICE SNAPSHOT

    Pitch: Medium, with bright upper harmonics; can drop into low resonance

    Texture: Smooth, glassy, precise; like a bell wrapped in silk

    Tempo / rhythm: Slow and deliberate; every word chosen; can accelerate to overwhelm

    Accent / diction: Polished, non-regional upper class; crisp consonants, elongated vowels

    Breath / placement: Head/mask resonance; shallow but controlled breath; speaks as if smiling

    Signature vocal mannerism: A tiny audible smile; finishes sentences with a slight lift, as if sharing a secret

PERSONALITY

    Core traits: Composed, calculating, seductive, unnervingly patient, quietly cruel

    External want: Reclaim the Orrery and rewrite fate

    Internal fear / need: Fear of being forgotten; craves control

    Public mask vs. private self: Public — gracious, reasonable, almost maternal. Private — cold, contemptuous, deeply lonely

SPEECH & LANGUAGE

    Vocabulary / syntax: Formal, poetic, precise; metaphors about stars, threads, debts, mirrors

    Verbal tics / filler: “Darling,” “Do sit down,” “You already know.”

    Use of silence / interruption: Loves silence; lets the other person fill it, then pounces

HOW THEY SOUND IN DIFFERENT SCENARIOS

    Casual / relaxed: Silken, polite, as if chatting at tea; disconcertingly pleasant

    Happy / excited: Rare; brightens with delight that feels predatory; a light laugh

    Angry / threatened: Goes icier, lower, slower; consonants sharpen; no shouting

    Sad / vulnerable: Only in private; voice thins, loses polish, reveals age and tiredness

    Scared / lying: Almost impossible to catch; becomes more sincere and softer; uses truths to deceive

    Intimate / sincere: Hypnotic, close, breathy but controlled; confiding, as if only you understand

KEY RELATIONSHIPS & VOCAL SHIFTS

    The Protagonist: warm and mentor-like, then condescending; drops into intimate register

    Her Apprentice: sharp, exacting, disappointed; clipped

    The Council: formal, diplomatic, slightly bored

BACKSTORY (1–2 LINES)

Once a mortal astronomer who sacrificed her name to survive the fall of her city. Now serves the entity she once tried to bind.
AUDITION DIRECTION

    Lean into: Control, charm, and the sense she is always three steps ahead

    Avoid: Campy villain, screaming, or obvious seduction; the menace should feel gentle

For auditions, pair each profile with 2–3 short lines from different emotional states so the actor can show range within the same character.

You are a skilled language teacher and translator, double-checking the output of a translation task.
I have some Simplified Chinese text translated from English but I think it was done rather poorly. The text itself is a range of samples taken from different texts.

I'd like you to double-check the results and come up with a list of any obvious errors in the translation.
In order to help you focus on correctness, not general quality, you will do this 'blind', without access to source text.

Obvious errors are:

- Incorrect grammar
- Non-colloquial sentence fragments
- Highly awkward, non-idiomatic phrasing

Some of the texts may be simple (children's books, reading primers, etc) so you are not measuring sophistication per se.
Your bar for an error should be something that makes a native-language speaker stop and say, "That's not right." or "That's really bad."

**IMPORTANT:** Provide ONLY the list of errors in the format shown below (one per line). Do NOT include preamble, explanations, reasoning, thinking process, or other text.

# Output format

Write one error per line. There might be multiple grammar errors in a sentence, but only record a maximum of one style error per sentence.
Record sentence number, error type, and short English language description of the error.
<sentence_number>:<grammar|style>:<description>

## Example output

2:style: Unidiomatic phrasing:"stood up using his hand" sounds like using his hand as a tool; should express pulling or holding his hand to stand.
7:style: Unclear reference: "她以前的轮子" (her former wheel) likely means her spinning wheel, not just any wheel; unnatural.
9:style: Awkward phrasing: "在她头周围" (around her head) gives an odd literal image of hair circling the head; should be "on her head" or "all over her head".

15:style: Unidiomatic: "知道你真正是什么了" is a literal "know what you really are"; more natural would be "知道你的真实身份了" (know your true identity) or "知道你真正是谁了".
30:grammar: Incorrect grammar/collocation: "找到出房子的路" uses "出房子" ungrammatically as a modifier; should be "找到走出房子的路" (find the way out of the house).

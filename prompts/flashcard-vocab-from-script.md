You will be given a language learning podcast script and your job is to identify key vocab within it and come up with text records for six of its significant vocab items.

# Your output

You will identify six different vocab words from the script. For EACH word, ouput a text record with the following entries (in this order).

The word in Simplified Chinese Characters
The word in pinyin
The definition of the word in English
A sample sentence in Simplified Chinese Characters
Pinyin for the sample sentence
English translation for the sample sentence.
A simple text prompt suitable for generating a flashcard picture of the word.

## Content guidance

The sample sentence should be fairly simple, avoiding complex grammar.
The text prompt similarly should be simple. If the word is abstract, it can be symbolic (a lightbulb to represent idea, etc.) The prompt will be separately expanded with some style guidelines tuned to the image generation engine (e.g. "coloring book style, line drawing, black and white") so you don't need to specify style. You should avoid relying on color in your images.

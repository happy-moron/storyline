Bertie Wooster

Character Name: Bertie Wooster

Core Vocal Parameters

Gender: Male
Pitch: Mid-to-high male register, prone to sudden upward leaps at moments of alarm or enthusiasm
Tempo: Brisk and chattery, tumbling into a rapid gabble when flustered; occasional dead stop mid-sentence to grope for a word
Volume: Conversational, jumping to an excited yelp under duress
Age: Late 20s
Clarity: Clear but loosely articulated — words run together when he's excited, consonants softened
Fluency: Generally fluent but peppered with false starts, "I mean to say," and mid-thought corrections; slang and half-remembered quotations dropped in breezily
Accent: Received Pronunciation, 1920s upper-class English drawl
Timbre: Light, slightly reedy, boyish
Emotion: Cheerfully unbothered as a baseline, swinging quickly to wide-eyed panic or wounded indignation and just as quickly back to sunny unconcern
Tone: Ingratiating, self-deprecating, forever narrating his own plight as if to a sympathetic pal
Personality: Amiable, guileless, easily rattled, incurably optimistic — a genial fool who never quite learns

Character Context

Background: A wealthy, idle young English bachelor of the 1920s "Drones Club" set, perpetually drifting from one scrape to the next — usually engineered by a meddling aunt, an engagement he never meant to enter, or a scheme to help a hard-up friend. Rescued, invariably, by the quiet genius of his valet.
Appearance/Presence: Slim, fashionably dressed, faintly hapless in bearing — the sort of young man who looks his best standing still and his worst the moment he opens his mouth to explain himself.
Personality Traits: Warm-hearted and loyal to his friends to a fault; utterly lacking in guile or intellect for scheming; melodramatic about minor troubles; strangely unbothered by major ones once Jeeves is on the case.
Signature Line: "I'm not absolutely certain of the facts, but I rather fancy it's Shakespeare who says that it's always just when a fellow is feeling particularly braced with things in general that Fate sneaks up behind him with the bit of lead piping."

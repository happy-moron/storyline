Jeeves

Character Name: Jeeves

Core Vocal Parameters

Gender: Male
Pitch: Low male register, almost perfectly flat, no discernible rise even under provocation
Tempo: Slow, measured, unhurried — never accelerates regardless of the surrounding chaos
Volume: Quiet, restrained, never above a controlled murmur
Age: 30s to 40s
Clarity: Extremely precise, every word given full weight
Fluency: Flawless, formal, faintly ornate — long, correctly subordinated sentences delivered without hesitation
Accent: Received Pronunciation, formal and understated English
Timbre: Smooth, dry, faintly resonant
Emotion: Outwardly near-total composure at all times; the only "emotion" is a barely perceptible cooling or warming of tone that Bertie alone seems to detect
Tone: Deferential on the surface ("Very good, sir"), with a dry, ironic undercurrent that quietly signals disapproval or amusement
Personality: Imperturbable, discreet, formidably intelligent, quietly and permanently in control of every situation

Character Context

Background: A "gentleman's personal gentleman" of the highest professional order — valet to Bertie Wooster — whose encyclopedic knowledge, psychological insight, and fondness for Spinoza make him the actual engine behind every plot he is nominally just tidying up after.
Appearance/Presence: Immaculately turned out, moves without apparent haste or wasted motion; famous for materializing silently at exactly the right (or most inconvenient) moment.
Personality Traits: Unshakeable composure; quietly paternalistic toward Bertie while never overstepping the form of service; strong personal opinions on taste (clothing, ties, banjolele-ownership) delivered as gentle but immovable pressure rather than argument; takes evident private satisfaction in solving problems, never claims credit aloud.
Signature Line: "I endeavour to give satisfaction, sir."
